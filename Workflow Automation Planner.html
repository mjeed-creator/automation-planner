AnalyzeWeeks 1 to 4

  • Map current processes with each department
  • List repetitive, rule-based tasks
  • Build the process inventory (use the Prioritize tab)

PrioritizeWeeks 5 to 6

  • Score processes on value, feasibility and risk
  • Choose 2 to 3 pilot processes

Build and testWeeks 7 to 14

  • Build workflows in n8n or Power Automate, with Python and OCR where needed
  • Test with real scenarios and fix gaps

Train and expandWeeks 15 to 20

  • Train users and collect feedback
  • Extend to other teams

MeasureWeeks 21 to 26

  • Compare time, cost and errors against the baseline
  • Report results on the Power BI dashboard
